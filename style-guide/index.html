A COMMON IDENTITY. ROOM TO CREATE.

One campaign.
Many natural expressions.

Give AI a creative direction, then let it design. Keep the mood, palette and visual character recognizable across posts, languages and social formats.

These are starting points, not a layout specification. AI generation does not use fixed font sizes, pixel grids, mandatory margins or a separate logo shelf.

THE SHARED FOUNDATION

A light foundation, with character.

Use white, warm white or pale brand tints as the overall background. Keep dark colors for readable text and small accents. Start with cyan and red. Use the supporting tones where they feel natural, balanced with your photographs and whitespace. Choose your own effective palette in Studio.

Core colors: #00A6EB and #E60036. Supporting colors: #81D0F4, #EF958C, #2A668E, #937C8A, #302A40, #951B3C.

CONSISTENCY WITHOUT A TEMPLATE

Design a family, not identical frames.

01

Share a visual identity

One campaign mood, typographic personality, photo treatment and family of motifs. The planner carries that identity through every requested post and format.

02

Let the story develop

Open with a useful idea, add complementary details and end with useful event facts. Change composition naturally within the same visual language.

03

Integrate the brand

Give the model your original logos and assigned photo. Let it integrate them into one finished design. The app adds no extra frame or duplicate logo block.

04

Adapt to the conversation

Square carousels, immersive Stories, welcoming Facebook announcements and practical Chat cards can share one look. Adapt the format and emphasis, not the entire brand aesthetic.

HELPFUL WORDS. FLEXIBLE LENGTHS.

Write for the reader.

A relevant opening, natural language, useful event details and an informative ending. Current public writing research can inform the text; sources and the check date appear in Studio. Without fresh evidence, the app uses baseline advice without claiming live trends.

Editorial starting ranges for event announcements — not hard limits
